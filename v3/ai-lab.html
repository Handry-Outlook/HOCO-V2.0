AI HOCO learns which weather models to trust

It checks past forecasts against observed lightning, is trained here first, goes live once it beats Traditional HOCO on runs it has never seen, then is re-checked every day. Traditional HOCO is never changed by anything on this page.

What happens next
–

Launch checklist

AI HOCO can be published when every check passes.

Controls

How AI HOCO scores against Traditional HOCO on runs it was not trained on. Lower Brier and log loss are better; higher AUC is better.

Setups and seasons

Average chance of lightning where the models show storms, by low-level flow and season. This is where AI HOCO corrects Traditional HOCO's habits, for example over-forecasting inland in cold-season westerlies.

What the weather context does

Learned effects (log-odds). Positive raises the chance of lightning, negative lowers it.

Learning progress

Training data collected, and AI HOCO's improvement over Traditional HOCO (Brier score, held-out runs).

Live verification

Once AI HOCO is live, both products are scored against observed lightning in the same way.

Model update log

After publishing, the trainer runs daily. A day's model is only replaced if the new one is no worse than the live one on the freshest verified runs.
