What we keep about you

  • Your account: your email address, your account type (for example Normal or Exclusive) and when you signed up. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase.
  • Your runs: the manual runs and ensembles you create, with their time window, region and settings, so you can open them again.
  • Access requests: if you ask for more access, the link or handle and the reason you give.

We don't sell your data. Private runs are visible only to you and to HOCO developers unless a developer makes a run public.

To delete your account or your runs, contact Handry Outlook and we'll remove them.

Cookies and local storage

Essential (always on): keeps you signed in and remembers your theme, region, map layers and saved place on this device.

Usage statistics (optional): anonymous counts of which features are used. Off unless you allow it.

Advertising (optional): for future ad spaces. Off unless you allow it.

You can change these at any time from your account menu → Cookie settings.

Services HOCO uses

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, database and file storage.
  • Mapbox: the base map and place search. Your searches are sent to Mapbox.
  • Windy: observed rain radar tiles, when you turn radar on.
  • Met Office and other public feeds: observed lightning strikes, when you turn strikes on.
  • GitHub: hosting this website and running the forecast jobs.

Terms of use

HOCO forecasts are automated guidance for interest and planning. They can be wrong. For warnings and safety decisions, always use your national weather service (in the UK, the Met Office).

You may share HOCO images and links with credit to Handry Outlook. Don't use HOCO to mislead people, and don't try to get around account limits.
